AttractCarsBot

Crawler information — Attractor Oy, Finland (business ID 3592322-7)

AttractCarsBot is a crawler operated by Attractor Oy. If you found it in your server logs, this page is for you.

It collects factual vehicle-listing data for AttractCars, a search service that helps people find cars for sale and sends them to the seller's own page to continue. Attractor is not a marketplace and never handles the sale.

Identifying it

Every request it makes carries both of these headers:

User-Agent: AttractCarsBot/1.0 (+https://attractorhq.github.io/attractcarsbot/)
From: info@attractor.fi

Please identify it by that user agent rather than by address: we publish no fixed egress IP range, and we would rather say so than publish a range you cannot rely on. We do not disguise the crawler, and we do not use residential proxies, IP rotation or any other form of identity evasion.

Controlling AttractCarsBot

It implements the Robots Exclusion Protocol (RFC 9309) and checks your robots.txt before every request. To stop it entirely:

User-agent: AttractCarsBot
Disallow: /

To allow it but ask it to slow down:

User-agent: AttractCarsBot
Crawl-delay: 10

It also honours text-and-data-mining reservations under Article 4 of the EU DSM Directive in any machine-readable form — a /.well-known/tdmrep.json reservation, a TDM-Reservation: 1 response header, or the equivalent meta tag. A reservation stops it regardless of what robots.txt says. It never attempts to bypass a CAPTCHA, a login, a paywall or a bot wall.

What AttractCarsBot stores

Only the factual fields that let somebody find a car: make, model, variant, year and first registration, mileage, price and currency, fuel, transmission, body type, power, colour, the country and town the car is in, and the URL of the listing so buyers can be sent to it.

It deliberately does not store:

  • Listing descriptions. Descriptive text is yours; only a hash is kept, so changes can be detected without copying the words.
  • Photographs, unless the recorded rights for that source permit it. By default images are neither copied nor hot-linked, and anti-framing and hotlink controls are respected.
  • Personal data. Professional and dealer inventory is indexed; private sellers' names, phone numbers, email addresses and home locations are not collected.

Opting out, corrections and takedowns

A robots.txt rule takes effect the next time the file is read. For anything else — a takedown, a correction, a rate complaint, or a question about what is stored — write to info@attractor.fi and a person will answer. Removal requests are honoured whether or not robots.txt is changed.
